## Product description

A practical copy-and-paste prompt library for creators who want better ideas, stronger scripts, faster repurposing, clearer SEO, more useful engagement, smarter analytics, and a path from content to revenue. Use it with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or another writing assistant. Replace bracketed fields and add your own experience before publishing.

## Usage instructions

1. Pick the category that matches your bottleneck.
2. Replace every bracket with your niche, audience, offer, platform, evidence, and constraints.
3. Add three voice adjectives and a short sample of your writing.
4. Ask for a draft first, then use a separate prompt to critique and revise it.
5. Add human proof, check facts and rights, and remove anything generic or unsupported.
6. Repurpose only after the core idea is clear; adapt hooks and pacing to each platform.
7. Track one learning metric per post and use the analytics prompts for the next iteration.

## 56 useful prompts

### Ideation

1. List 15 urgent, recurring problems for [audience] in [niche], including the audience language, a mistaken belief, and one helpful content angle for each.
2. Build four content pillars around this point of view: [belief]. For each give a promise, three subtopics, five post ideas, proof types, and one off-brand topic.
3. Turn [core idea] into ten angles: how-to, teardown, myth, story, comparison, checklist, opinion, case study, beginner lesson, and prediction.
4. Create a seven-step ladder teaching [topic] from beginner to advanced; include prerequisite, common mistake, example, and best format for each step.
5. Cluster these audience questions [questions] and turn the strongest into a 30-day backlog with intent, answer, format, hook, and helpful CTA.
6. Interview me with 12 questions that uncover failures, turning points, customer moments, trade-offs, and repeatable lessons from [field], then turn answers into story briefs.
7. Evaluate [trend] for fit with [niche]. Score relevance, shelf life, credibility risk, and speed, then give three original angles or a reason to skip it.

### Scripting

8. Write a [platform] script teaching [skill] to [audience] with hook, promise, three steps, example, caveat, CTA, visual cues, and a [length] limit.
9. Turn [experience] into a story-led script that starts in tension, uses one sensory detail, delays the lesson, and ends with a practical principle and CTA.
10. Answer this objection [objection] about [offer] by acknowledging its valid part, explaining when it is true, showing an alternative, and avoiding pressure or guarantees.
11. Turn these case-study notes [notes] into a credible script with starting point, intervention, timeframe, result, limitations, evidence cues, and a transferable lesson.
12. Write a defensible contrarian script about [claim]: state conventional advice, where it breaks, the better rule, and the exception; flag claims needing sources.
13. Explain [complex concept] to a smart beginner using one analogy, plain definitions, one example, a three-item recap, and a note about likely confusion.
14. Edit this draft [draft] to match this voice sample [sample]. Return a light edit, sharper edit, cuts with reasons, and lines that sound generic.

### Short-form video

15. Create 25 truthful hooks for [topic] and [audience], five each for curiosity, result, mistake, story, and opinion; add visual opening and fulfilled promise.
16. Turn [idea] into a 30-second vertical shot list with timestamps, spoken line, on-screen text, camera direction, caption, and a phone-only version.
17. Diagnose drop-offs in this script [script], then rewrite the opening, pacing, pattern changes, and payoff while explaining each meaningful edit.
18. Design five silent videos about [topic] with first-frame text, 5–7 visual beats, captions, accessibility description, and final CTA.
19. Select six useful comments from [comments] and outline respectful 20–40 second reply videos that answer, add nuance, and give a next step.
20. Adapt [idea] for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, and LinkedIn with platform-specific hook, pace, text, caption, CTA, and one change per platform.
21. Turn [topics] into a batch filming plan grouped by setup, with briefs, props, preflight checklist, file names, retakes, and reset breaks.

### Repurposing

22. Extract a map from [transcript]: thesis, five insights, three stories, objections, quotes, missing context, format, platform, hook, source marker, and CTA.
23. Convert [newsletter] into an [N]-slide carousel with slide copy, practical example, final CTA, alt text, and caption without losing nuance.
24. From [podcast transcript], select ten standalone 20–60 second clips with exact markers, hook, first-frame text, edit note, and context warning.
25. Build a seven-day plan from [pillar]: long-form anchor, two videos, email, text post, carousel, and community prompt, explaining how assets connect.
26. Turn [case study] into a proof block, email, video, social post, FAQ, and testimonial request while preserving limitations and permission needs.
27. Refresh [old content] by identifying outdated claims, missing examples, and structural improvements; return a change log and refreshed version.
28. Quality-check [assets] for claim consistency, facts, terms, CTA, voice, accessibility, platform fit, and any item that must wait for verification.

### SEO

29. Create an SEO brief for [keyword] covering intent, reader job, questions, differentiated angle, outline, examples, and claims that need sources.
30. Build a keyword cluster around [topic], grouped by learn, compare, solve, and buy; designate a canonical page, support pages, and wrong-audience terms.
31. Write 12 accurate title tags and eight meta descriptions for [page] targeting [keyword], labeled by angle, with the strongest pair recommended.
32. Draft a helpful article on [topic] for [audience] that leads with the answer, uses clear headings, gives an actionable process, examples, limits, and source markers.
33. Generate ten real FAQs and plain-language answers for [topic], flagging anything that needs policy, price, or claim confirmation.
34. Audit [page] for intent, first-paragraph answer, freshness, examples, headings, links, title/meta fit, accessibility, and conversion path; prioritize fixes.
35. Rewrite [draft] for topical clarity using [terms] naturally, preserving voice and experience, and list terms you intentionally did not force.

### Engagement

36. Write ten captions for [topic] that invite thoughtful replies using specific questions, choices, experiences, disagreements, and implementation check-ins.
37. Create concise response templates for praise, question, correction, disagreement, story, self-promotion, spam, and misinformation; say when not to reply.
38. Design a 30-minute community discussion on [topic] with context, three questions, exercise, inclusion method, moderation boundaries, and closing action.
39. Write a ten-question unbiased audience survey about [problem], mixing multiple choice, ranking, and open response; state what each answer informs.
40. Turn [position] into a civil debate post with belief, strongest opposing case, reasonable uncertainty, and a question without bait or caricature.
41. Draft a low-pressure collaboration DM, email, and public reply for [creator type] around [topic], showing audience benefit and an easy no.
42. Analyze [feedback] into issues, requests, compliments, misunderstandings, and outliers; recommend five content responses and a private follow-up.

### Analytics

43. Diagnose this post using [metrics] versus [baseline]; identify bottleneck, unknowns, two hypotheses, and one next change without claiming causation.
44. Interpret this retention data [data], locating opening drop, strongest section, and exits; give explanations, an edit test, and a sample-size caution.
45. Design a two-week content experiment for [goal] with hypothesis, variable, control, sample plan, primary metric, guardrails, threshold, and decision rule.
46. Build a weekly scorecard for [platforms] with definitions, data source, owner, cadence, leading metrics, lagging metrics, and a decision narrative.
47. Compare top and low performers [posts and metrics] for topic, hook, length, proof, CTA, and intent; separate repeatable patterns from luck and brief five tests.
48. Audit the path from [post] to [landing page] using [conversion data] for mismatch, friction, trust, tracking, and the smallest high-confidence fix.
49. Turn [monthly data] into an honest review of gains, declines, audience learning, changed assumptions, priorities, and what to stop doing.

### Monetization

50. Shape three offer formats for validated problem [problem] and audience [audience], with deliverable, scope, price-testing logic, proof, objections, and no-pressure CTA.
51. Write five value propositions for [offer] serving [audience], each with specific outcome, difference, supporting subhead, proof ideas, and evidence level.
52. Plan a seven-day launch for [product] at [price] with problem, proof, objection, reveal, reminder, and follow-up; use only genuine urgency.
53. Create five educational posts that lead transparently to [offer], each useful without purchase and clear about the commercial CTA.
54. Ask 15 questions needed for pricing and packaging [offer] covering alternatives, cost, proof, scope, support, repeatability, and willingness-to-pay evidence.
55. Write an honest testimonial request for [offer] asking about before, decision, experience, specific change, fit, mixed feedback, and publishing permission.
56. Write checkout copy for [product] at [price] covering inclusions, audience, delivery, support, refund terms provided, one limitation, and five truthful FAQs.
