# Fourteen lane workflows

These are the fourteen named lanes: seven vices and seven virtues. Each line is a starter description, not a live trading or automation claim.

1. **Pride** — quality-control lane for reviewing assumptions, outputs, and overconfidence before release.
2. **Greed** — opportunity lane for ranking upside ideas while enforcing explicit risk and budget limits.
3. **Lust** — attention lane for turning strong hooks and visual ideas into ethical, consent-aware creative tests.
4. **Envy** — research lane for studying competitors and extracting lessons without copying protected work.
5. **Gluttony** — volume lane for batching excess ideas, then pruning them into a focused weekly queue.
6. **Wrath** — incident lane for escalating failures, suspicious activity, and broken safeguards safely.
7. **Sloth** — simplification lane for removing repetitive manual steps without weakening review gates.
8. **Humility** — learning lane for recording uncertainty, corrections, and feedback from real outcomes.
9. **Charity** — service lane for creating useful, accessible outputs for customers and the community.
10. **Chastity** — boundaries lane for protecting consent, privacy, credentials, and appropriate data use.
11. **Gratitude** — retention lane for acknowledging customers, collaborators, and useful signals.
12. **Temperance** — balance lane for controlling frequency, spend, leverage, and automation intensity.
13. **Patience** — validation lane for waiting for enough evidence before promoting a strategy or launch.
14. **Diligence** — execution lane for checklists, documentation, testing, and dependable follow-through.
