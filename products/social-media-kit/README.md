# Local Business Social Media Kit

A practical Canva Free template pack for local businesses that post every day but do not have time to design from scratch.

## Why this product

Mel is 19, based in Nairobi, and already builds websites for local businesses at **KSh 9,000 with a 3-day turnaround**. Working with real business owners makes the problem clear: the service may be good, but posting consistently can feel like another full-time job. This kit turns the most common daily posts—offers, services, testimonials, opening hours, events, and reminders—into repeatable layouts.

Mel also uses Canva daily, so these templates are designed around a quick, realistic workflow in Canva Free: duplicate a page, swap the logo and colors, update the copy, and export. Canva templates are a strong fit for TikTok-marketed digital products because a short before/after or “steal this template” video can show the value immediately while sending interested viewers to a simple purchase link.

This is not a promise of followers, sales, or overnight results. It is a clean starting system for showing up more consistently and looking more professional.

## What is included

- A build guide for exactly 10 Canva Free-friendly templates.
- Social post layouts for square feeds, carousels, and stories.
- Captions, launch copy, listing guidance, and a Nairobi-focused TikTok promotion plan.
- A simple cross-sell angle for Mel’s website clients.

## Who it is for

Salons, barbers, cafés, restaurants, boutiques, beauty professionals, tutors, fitness studios, repair shops, photographers, and other local businesses that need useful content without hiring a designer for every post.

## Quick-start workflow

1. Open Canva and create a design in the format you need: Instagram Post (Square) for feed posts or Instagram Story for reminders.
2. Build the 10 layouts from `canva-template-guide.md` using only assets with Canva’s **Free** label.
3. Create one mini brand kit: logo, two colors, one accent color, and two fonts.
4. Duplicate the finished pages, replace the sample copy, and export PNGs or MP4s where appropriate.
5. Use the sales copy and promotion plan to list and introduce the kit.
