DESCRIPTION (150 WORDS)

Trading discipline is not a signal service, a shortcut, or a promise of profit. The KYLA Scalper’s Discipline Playbook is a practical, beginner-friendly system for turning a trading plan into repeatable actions. It is built around a simple real-world framework: no more than three trades from Monday to Thursday, a 45-minute maximum hold, a defined per-trade guard, a $150 weekly trailing target on a $5,000 funded account, and a hard kill-switch when your rules say stop. Inside, you’ll find a pre-trade checklist, journaling template, post-trade review questions, risk boundaries, and mindset prompts for trading with ADHD or anxiety. Use it to slow down, document decisions, and protect your attention and capital. This is education, not financial advice, and it does not guarantee results. Download the playbook, adapt the templates to your own rules, and build a process you can actually follow without relying on hype, urgency, or someone else’s results.
