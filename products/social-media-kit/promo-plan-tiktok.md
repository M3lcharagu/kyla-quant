**Audience:** Local-business owners who post often but struggle to design consistently.  
**CTA for every video:** “Get the Local Business Social Media Kit—link in bio.”

Use the times below as starting points, clearly labelled in your scheduling notes as **Africa/Nairobi time (EAT, UTC+3)**. Review watch time, saves, comments, and link clicks after posting; do not promise a specific result.

## TikTok 1 — The daily-content problem

- **Hook/format:** `POV: ur local business has no content`
- **Format:** A quick talking-head or desk video. Open with the exact on-screen text, cut to a blank Canva page and a messy list of post ideas, then show the 10-page kit overview.
- **Shot list:** 0–2 seconds: point at the blank page. 2–6 seconds: show the service, offer, testimonial, and open-now pages. 6–10 seconds: show one finished example with a logo and brand colours. 10–13 seconds: point to the bio.
- **Voiceover:** “You know you should post, but every design starts from zero. I built a simple Canva Free kit for the posts local businesses use most. Customise it with your logo, then export and post.”
- **Posting time:** **Tuesday, 7:30 p.m. Africa/Nairobi (EAT, UTC+3).**
- **CTA:** “Get the Local Business Social Media Kit—link in bio.”

## TikTok 2 — Before/after slides

- **Hook/format:** `before/after slides`
- **Format:** Use a fast slideshow with the first frame labelled “Before: blank canvas” and the second labelled “After: branded post.” Show three transformations: offer, testimonial, and price list. Add a small “made in Canva Free” label only if every shown asset is Free.
- **Shot list:** 0–1 seconds: blank page. 1–4 seconds: offer before/after. 4–7 seconds: testimonial before/after. 7–10 seconds: price list before/after. 10–13 seconds: show the kit cover and the purchase direction.
- **Voiceover/text:** “Same business details, clearer structure. Add your colours, logo, and real information, then make the design fit your brand.”
- **Posting time:** **Thursday, 12:30 p.m. Africa/Nairobi (EAT, UTC+3).**
- **CTA:** “See the full Local Business Social Media Kit—link in bio.”

## TikTok 3 — Template tip and soft sell

- **Hook/format:** `steal this template`
- **Format:** Screen-record the build of the “open now” post. Duplicate a page, change the accent colour, replace the hours and location, add the logo, and preview at phone size. Keep the demo business clearly fictional or use a client example only with permission.
- **Shot list:** 0–2 seconds: show the finished template with the hook on screen. 2–6 seconds: change the headline and hours. 6–9 seconds: add the logo and accent. 9–12 seconds: show the phone preview and a final CTA card.
- **Voiceover:** “Steal this template structure: one clear status, today’s hours, where to find you, and one action. Make it yours with accurate business details, then save it for your next opening-hours update.”
- **Posting time:** **Saturday, 10:00 a.m. Africa/Nairobi (EAT, UTC+3).**
- **CTA:** “Get all 10 layouts in the Local Business Social Media Kit—link in bio.”
