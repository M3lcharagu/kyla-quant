# Canva Free Template Build Guide

Build these as a small, consistent system rather than ten unrelated designs. Start with **Instagram Post (Square)** at 1080 × 1080 px for posts and **Instagram Story** at 1080 × 1920 px for the story template. Duplicate pages as you work. Every suggested asset below should be checked for Canva’s **Free** label—do not use a Pro crown asset.

## 1. Logo-reveal post

- **Purpose:** Introduce a new logo, rebrand, or memorable visual identity without making the announcement feel crowded.
- **Layout:** Use a solid or lightly textured background. Place the logo large in the centre, a small “Meet our new look” eyebrow above it, and the business name plus one short descriptor below. Keep generous space around the mark.
- **Colors/fonts:** Use the brand’s main color for the background and a high-contrast neutral for text. Pair **Poppins SemiBold** for the name with **DM Sans Regular** for the descriptor; both are easy to brand with different weights.
- **Canva Free:** Add the logo through **Uploads**; choose a plain shape from **Elements → Shapes**; add copy through **Text**. Use **Position → Center** and the spacing controls instead of decorative Pro graphics.
- **Caption example:** “New look, same care—meet the refreshed [Business Name]. What do you think?”

## 2. Promo/offer posts

- **Purpose:** Make a time-bound offer easy to understand at a glance.
- **Layout:** Put a short “This week” label at the top, the offer in the largest type, one supporting line underneath, and a bold CTA button or footer with the booking/contact method. Include a small date or terms line so the offer is clear.
- **Colors/fonts:** Use one accent color only for the offer and CTA; keep the background neutral. Pair **Montserrat ExtraBold** for the offer with **Open Sans Regular** for details.
- **Canva Free:** Build the CTA with **Elements → Shapes** and add a free line or dot only if needed. Use **Text → Add a heading** for the offer and **Text → Add a little bit of body text** for dates and terms. Search **Elements** for “free sparkle” only when the result has no Pro crown.
- **Caption example:** “Book [service] by [date] and enjoy [offer]. Send us a DM or call [number] to reserve.”

## 3. Service highlight carousel

- **Purpose:** Explain one service in a swipeable sequence so a potential customer knows what it includes.
- **Layout:** Create four to five slides: cover with the service name, “what you get,” three short benefit points, process or timing, and a final CTA. Keep the same top label, footer, and slide number on every page.
- **Colors/fonts:** Choose one dark base, one light background, and one brand accent. Use **League Spartan Bold** for slide titles and **Lato Regular** for body copy; vary weight, not font count.
- **Canva Free:** Duplicate the first page to preserve alignment. Use **Elements → Shapes** for cards and **Text** for the slide number. Search **Elements** for a simple free icon that matches the service, or use the business’s own photo through **Uploads**.
- **Caption example:** “Wondering what’s included in [service]? Swipe through, then message us for a suitable option.”

## 4. Before-after post

- **Purpose:** Show a transformation or finished result with context, without making an unrealistic promise.
- **Layout:** Split the canvas into two equal panels with “Before” and “After” labels. Add a small bottom bar for the service, date, or process note. Use original photos with similar framing when possible.
- **Colors/fonts:** Keep the photo area clean and use the brand accent for the divider and labels. Pair **Bebas Neue** for compact labels with **DM Sans** for the supporting note.
- **Canva Free:** Add photos via **Uploads**; use **Elements → Shapes** for the two-panel background and divider. Crop with **Edit photo → Crop**. Avoid stock images that imply a client result you cannot substantiate.
- **Caption example:** “A fresh [service] for [client/project]. Results vary by starting point—message us to discuss your goals.”

## 5. Testimonial post

- **Purpose:** Let a real customer’s words build context and trust.
- **Layout:** Put a short quote in a large text block, the customer’s first name or approved identifier below, and a small service label at the top. Leave breathing room and keep the quote to two or three lines on the main page.
- **Colors/fonts:** Use a calm light background, dark text, and a single warm accent for quotation marks or a rule. Pair **Cormorant Garamond SemiBold** for the quote with **Montserrat Regular** for the attribution.
- **Canva Free:** Add a free quotation-mark glyph from **Text** or use a basic **Elements → Shapes** circle behind it. Import a customer photo only with permission; otherwise use the quote alone. Check the final text against the approved testimonial.
- **Caption example:** ““[Short approved quote].” — [First name], after [service]. Thank you for choosing [Business Name].”

## 6. Event announcement

- **Purpose:** Give people the essential details for a class, pop-up, opening, sale, or community event.
- **Layout:** Lead with the event name, then use three clear information rows for date, time, and location/online link. Put the RSVP action in a contrasting footer and add one relevant image or simple shape, not several competing graphics.
- **Colors/fonts:** Use the event’s accent as a border or header and keep the information area white or cream. Pair **Poppins Bold** for the event name with **Roboto Regular** for details.
- **Canva Free:** Use **Elements → Shapes** for the information rows and search **Elements** for free calendar, clock, and location icons. Add the event image through **Uploads** or pick a free photo from **Elements → Photos**.
- **Caption example:** “Join us for [event] on [date] at [time] in [location]. RSVP by [method]—we would love to see you there.”

## 7. “Open now” post

- **Purpose:** Remove uncertainty by telling nearby customers that the business is open and how to visit or order.
- **Layout:** Make “OPEN NOW” the hero line inside a bold badge or rounded card. Under it, show today’s hours, location or delivery area, and one action such as “Walk in,” “Order,” or “Book.”
- **Colors/fonts:** Choose a clear green or brand accent for the open badge, paired with a deep neutral for readable details. Use **Archivo Black** for the hero line and **Open Sans** for hours.
- **Canva Free:** Create the badge with **Elements → Shapes → Rounded rectangle**; add a free arrow or map-pin icon only if useful. Use **Text** for hours and align all details with **Position → Tidy up**.
- **Caption example:** “We’re open today from [time] to [time]. Find us at [location] or message [handle] before you come.”

## 8. Price list carousel

- **Purpose:** Present services and starting prices in a scannable format while leaving room for terms or custom quotes.
- **Layout:** Use a cover, one or two category slides with three to five items each, and a final contact slide. Set each item as a consistent row: service name on the left, price on the right, with a small note for “from,” duration, or what is included.
- **Colors/fonts:** Use a dark text color, light background, and one accent for category headers. Pair **Montserrat SemiBold** for service names with **Lato Regular** for notes and prices.
- **Canva Free:** Use **Elements → Shapes** for thin dividers and text boxes for rows. Duplicate each category page. Add “Prices valid from [date]” in a small but readable footer and confirm all figures before export.
- **Caption example:** “Here’s a quick guide to our current [service] prices. Save this post, then message us for availability or a custom quote.”

## 9. Thank-you post

- **Purpose:** Acknowledge customers, repeat visits, referrals, or a completed milestone in a warm, shareable way.
- **Layout:** Use a large “Thank you” headline, one specific appreciation sentence, and a small signature or handle. Add a soft photo or colour block on one side, keeping the message as the focus.
- **Colors/fonts:** Use the business’s lightest brand shade with a warm accent and dark text. Pair **Playfair Display Bold** for the headline with **DM Sans Regular** for the note.
- **Canva Free:** Add a customer-approved photo through **Uploads**, or use **Elements → Shapes** and a free abstract blob. Use **Text** for the headline and keep decorative searches limited to free lines, stars, or dots.
- **Caption example:** “Thank you for choosing [Business Name] and helping our small business grow. We appreciate you.”

## 10. Story/reminder template

- **Purpose:** Create a quick daily reminder for appointments, order cut-offs, restocks, deadlines, or a question sticker prompt.
- **Layout:** Use a vertical canvas with a top label, one large reminder sentence in the middle, a date/time line, and a bottom CTA. Leave the top and bottom edges clear for Instagram interface controls.
- **Colors/fonts:** Use a bold accent panel against a neutral background, with high contrast for accessibility. Pair **Nunito ExtraBold** for the reminder with **Roboto Regular** for the details.
- **Canva Free:** Choose **Instagram Story** from Canva’s design search. Use **Elements → Shapes** for the panel, **Text** for the reminder, and a free arrow or underline only when it improves direction. Add a logo from **Uploads** and keep it away from the top and bottom safe areas.
- **Caption example:** “Reminder: bookings for [day/service] close at [time]. Tap the link in our bio or DM us to reserve.”

## Final build check

- Use no Canva Pro assets, fonts, or effects if the product promise is Canva Free.
- Check mobile readability, spelling, dates, prices, handles, and contrast.
- Replace every placeholder before publishing.
- Export sample previews separately from the editable working file, and label demo content clearly.
