What changes

Less hindsight. More evidence.

  • Write the invalidation point and maximum loss before entry.
  • Size from a defined risk budget instead of emotional conviction or buying power.
  • Separate execution quality from whether price happened to go your way.
  • Find recurring behavior patterns across a meaningful sample.
  • Keep changes small enough to test and specific enough to review.

No. It does not provide signals, entries, exits, instruments, or recommendations. It is a process and record-keeping toolkit.

It lays out equity, risk budget, stop distance, value per unit, costs, and a rounded position size. Verify contract specifications and all math yourself.

No. Journaling cannot remove market risk or guarantee profit. It can make decisions and behavior easier to inspect.
