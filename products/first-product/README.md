# KYLA Scalper's Discipline Playbook

> **Risk disclaimer:** This is educational material, not financial advice or a promise of income. Trading involves substantial risk, including loss of capital. The rules and numbers below describe Mel's personal process reference and must be adapted to your own account, broker, prop-firm rules, and risk tolerance. No result is guaranteed.

## Product-choice rationale

Mel is a 19-year-old CS student in Kenya building KYLA around practical systems: websites, trading, and automation. The first product is deliberately a **discipline and risk-management playbook**, not a signal service. It turns the real setup Mel is working on into a useful, honest digital download:

- A maximum of 3 trades from Monday to Thursday.
- A 45-minute maximum hold.
- A tight, pre-defined per-trade guard that is never widened to rescue a position.
- A $150/week trailing target reference on a $5,000 funded account—not a promise or recommended expectation.
- Kill-switch rules, journaling, checklists, review questions, and mindset support for ADHD/anxiety.

This is a strong first product because it solves a specific process problem, can be used immediately, and does not depend on claiming that a strategy will make anyone profitable.
