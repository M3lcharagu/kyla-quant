# Orochi Framework research note

## Scope and evidence posture

Orochi is described in the supplied research as a branded, paid NQ-futures ecosystem associated with Thomas / `@deltatrendtrading`. The notes below record the claimed components and the implied process without treating the marketing or public material as proof of a trading edge.

## Claimed components

The claimed framework combines:

- Auction Market Theory.
- TPO / Market Profile.
- Volume Profile.
- VWAP.
- Prior-session value areas and POC.
- HVN / LVN.
- Session high and low.
- Footprint charts.
- Cumulative delta.
- Absorption and imbalances.
- DOM / tape reading.
- Market structure.

These are logged as distinct concepts, not as a verified single mechanical strategy.

## Implied workflow

The implied sequence is:

1. Establish the higher-timeframe (HTF) auction context.
2. Wait for price at a profile, value, or liquidity area.
3. Seek order-flow confirmation via absorption plus delta improvement or divergence.
4. Place stops beyond structural failure.
5. Target the opposing profile.

This is an interpretation of the supplied research, not a claim that the ecosystem publishes a complete, reproducible rule set.

## What was not found

No exact settings, entry thresholds, timeframes, stop/target rules, or sizing rules were found. No verifiable backtest was found. Accordingly, the components above cannot be treated as independently specified, reproducible, or validated by this note.

## Verdict

**SKIP for now.** Log the components as separate hypotheses for future testing. Never promote Orochi as a validated edge unless explicit rules, data, costs, and an independently reproducible out-of-sample evaluation are established.
