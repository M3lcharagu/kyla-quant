# KYLA Scalper's Discipline Playbook: 3-Day Launch Plan

> **Risk disclaimer:** The product and this launch plan are educational. Do not make profit promises, present personal targets as expected results, or give financial advice. Trading involves risk, and every buyer must adapt the material to their own circumstances.

## Launch objective

Publish one useful, honest digital product for traders who want stronger process discipline—not a signal service or a get-rich pitch. Start with **KSh 1,990 on Selar** for Kenya and **$19 on Gumroad** for global buyers. Keep the first test simple: one product, one clear promise, one link per platform, and one variable changed at a time.

## Day 1 — Set up Selar and Gumroad, then post

### Set up

- [ ] Confirm the final download is clean, readable on mobile, and includes the disclaimer at the top.
- [ ] Create or finish the Selar account. Upload the playbook, use the recommended title, select the closest Education / Investing & Trading category, set **KSh 1,990**, and configure the available Kenya payout route including M-PESA. Complete any verification and test the buyer flow.
- [ ] Create or finish the Gumroad account. Upload the same product, use the recommended title, set **$19 USD**, and complete available global checkout, payout, tax, and contact settings. Preview the page and download flow.
- [ ] Check that both listings clearly say educational, do not promise profit, and explain that the $150/week reference on a $5,000 funded account is personal context—not a forecast.

### Post

Publish one short announcement on the channels Mel already uses. Suggested copy:

> I turned my trading rules into the KYLA Scalper's Discipline Playbook: 3-trade limit, 45-minute hold, per-trade guard, kill switch, journal, and ADHD/anxiety-friendly review prompts. No signals or profit promises—just a process you can adapt. Link in bio.

Use one direct link per platform, save the post URL, and note the launch time. Do not manufacture scarcity or claim that buyers will earn money.

## Day 2 — Promote on TikTok and Instagram

Post from **TikTok `@lemiii1_`** and **Instagram `@lemmii1_`**. Keep the same core idea but tailor the format to each platform.

### Content sequence

1. **Hook:** “The rule that stops me from turning one bad trade into five.”
2. **Show:** A blurred or recreated checklist with the 3-trade limit, 45-minute maximum hold, and kill-switch line visible.
3. **Explain:** The product is about execution and risk boundaries, not signals or guaranteed profit.
4. **Invite:** “Comment ‘PLAYBOOK’ or use the link in bio to see the educational download.”

For TikTok, make a short vertical walkthrough of the checklist or journal. For Instagram, use a Reel plus a Story that points to the product page. Answer questions honestly; do not share a winning screenshot as proof of likely results. Label the content clearly as a product promotion where required.

Record views, watch time, saves, profile visits, link clicks, DMs, and questions separately for each platform. A low-sales day is data, not a reason to increase trading risk or make a stronger claim.

## Day 3 — First follow-up and iterate price

### Follow up

- [ ] Reply to useful comments and DMs with the product link and the honest disclaimer.
- [ ] Publish a follow-up showing one page of the journal or the post-trade questions.
- [ ] Re-state who it is for: traders building a personal process who want fewer impulsive decisions.
- [ ] Review Selar and Gumroad page views, checkout starts, purchases, questions, and refund requests. Compare them with TikTok and Instagram clicks.

### Iterate carefully

Keep the title and product stable for the first read of the data. If the page receives attention but few checkouts, test **one** change—price, first paragraph, or preview—not everything at once. Start with the recommended **KSh 1,990** and **$19**; if testing price, record the old price, new price, date, traffic source, and observation. Never use a fake deadline or discount that implies guaranteed trading success.

After the first follow-up, choose the next experiment from real questions buyers asked. Keep the risk disclaimer, educational positioning, and no-profit-promise standard in every revision.
